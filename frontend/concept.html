Why it’s different

Decision coach, not just a 3D replay

Decision coach

Flags key moments—early thermal exits, low saves, speedbar hesitations—and explains them in plain language.

Mindset & safety

Highlights risky patterns (tight turns below 100 m, late decision gates) and turns them into reusable rules.

Beginner-first

Starts with a simple summary; lets intermediates dig deeper into thermals, glides, and speed strategy when needed.

Local & private

Processing happens on your machine; you decide what to share with coaches or friends.

Questions we answer

The real questions pilots ask

How fast did I find lift, and why?

We measure time to first lift and show the triggers you chose.

Which thermals were worth it?

Counts thermals, climb quality, and wasted circles.

Where should I have used more bar?

Headwind, sink, and altitude margin cues to accelerate.

Did I make risky low turns?

Flags tight turns & decision gates under 150 m AGL.

Am I improving over my last flights?

Tracks climb consistency, early exits, and low-save frequency.

For pilots

Confidence & progression

  • • Clear takeaway after every flight without reading charts for hours.
  • • Simple rules to try next: “stay 2 more circles when vario ≥1.2 m/s”.
  • • Privacy-first: keep tracks local unless you choose to share.

For instructors

Faster, consistent feedback

  • • Share flights with annotations and voice notes tied to timepoints.
  • • Compare students on key decisions (thermal exits, low saves, speedbar use).
  • • Track progression: climbs, early exits, low-save quality across sessions.
